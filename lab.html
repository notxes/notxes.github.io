01 / Network engineering

Architecture & segmentation

OPNsense manages inter-VLAN routing and access controls, while managed switching separates systems by role. The goal is to make permitted traffic intentional and observable rather than operate one flat lab network.

Internet
OPNsense Firewall Routing · VPN · firewall policy
Managed Switching Cisco Catalyst · VLANs · SPAN
Management Infrastructure & administration
Red Team Controlled testing systems
Victim Lab Windows / Linux targets
Blue Team Monitoring & investigation
IoT Lower-trust devices
DMZ Design Isolated service tier

Simplified conceptual layout. Actual routing and trust boundaries depend on configured interfaces and firewall policies. Public IPs and sensitive rules are intentionally omitted.

What I configured

  • VLAN interfaces and routing through OPNsense.
  • Inter-VLAN allow/deny rules for lab zones.
  • Managed switch VLANs and traffic mirroring.
  • DNS, connectivity, and firewall troubleshooting.

What the design demonstrates

  • Separation of higher- and lower-trust workloads.
  • Practical firewall and switching administration.
  • Traffic visibility for defensive monitoring.
  • A repeatable environment for security research.
